## Facebook / LinkedIn (longer)

Still putting your websites online with FTP? Good. So do we.

We built coolFTP for the people who actually keep small business websites running: freelancers, consultants, marketers, the person at the agency who knows the FTP password. No git, no command line, no "workflow." Drag files across, the way you always have.

The difference is what happens after you drop them:

- It knows which files changed, and only sends those. No more comparing timestamps.
- Every file is confirmed by the server after it lands. No more half-uploaded pages.
- It opens your website afterwards to make sure it really loads.
- Overwrote the wrong page? Hit Undo. The previous version was kept on your server.

Free for Windows. Works with the hosting you already have (GoDaddy, Hostinger, Bluehost, SiteGround, anything with FTP or SFTP).
